Hotspot Messenger lets you chat with people nearby on the same Wi‑Fi or mobile hotspot — no internet or mobile data required.

<b>Perfect for:</b>
• Study groups at school or library
• Events, camps, and meetups
• Travel when you only have a local hotspot
• Any place where everyone shares one network

<b>Features:</b>
• Real-time group chat and private DMs
• Rooms and channels
• Share photos and files
• QR code to join quickly
• Scan or save server addresses
• Dark mode and mobile-friendly design
• Optional message encryption with a passphrase

<b>How it works:</b>
One device runs the chat server (laptop or Android with Termux). Everyone else opens the app, enters the local address or scans a QR code, picks a name, and starts chatting. All messages stay on your local network.

<b>Privacy:</b>
No cloud account. No ads. Your chats stay on the host device on your LAN. See our privacy policy in the app.

<b>Note:</b>
You need to be on the same Wi‑Fi or hotspot as the host. This is not an internet messaging app like WhatsApp.
