Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Hotspot Messenger is a local network chat app. Messages stay on your Wi‑Fi or hotspot. We do not run a cloud server and do not collect your chats on our servers.

Data on your device

  • Display name and avatar you choose
  • Saved server URL and app settings (notifications, theme)

Data on the host server

Whoever runs the server (a laptop or phone with Termux) stores chat history and uploaded files locally in the project data/ folder.

What we do not do

  • No ads or analytics SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No cloud account required
